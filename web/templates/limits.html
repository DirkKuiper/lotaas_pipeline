FRB rate limit

What the campaign can publish if it finds no new FRB: the 95% upper limit on the all-sky rate R(>F) of bursts above fluence F at 135 MHz, with N(>F) ∝ Fα. Every SAP searched since the FRB configuration of 28 September 2026 counts with its tied-array beams' positions, its length, its sky temperature (Haslam 408 MHz) and elevation, and the pulsar-calibrated sensitivity (SEFD 410 Jy at the zenith under a 350 K sky, ±25%). The chance that a burst reaches the review queue is measured, per release and source, on the bursts the injection lane puts through the whole pipeline. Population: DM log-uniform 100–3000, scattering log-uniform 1 ms – 3 s at 135 MHz. None found gives R < 3 / exposure. Recomputed daily at 06:20 UTC by python -m euroflash.frb_limits.
