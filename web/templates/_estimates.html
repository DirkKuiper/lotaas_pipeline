How progress and time estimates are calculated

The goal includes all survey, confirmation and unclassified observations in Cees Bassa’s catalogue. A SAP is one project/observation/SAP identity, regardless of archive processing version. It is complete only when every included beam archive has successful production searches. “In queue” means at least one archive is in the campaign inventory, including completed work; {{ '{:,}'.format(scope.in_campaign) }} of this scope’s {{ '{:,}'.format(scope.files) }} archives are in that inventory.

These are conditional projections for mapped beam archives, not finish dates. Retrieval and search overlap, so the headline takes the slower duration. Project estimates assume the full measured campaign capacity is devoted to that project. Rates include downtime; pilots, failed searches and repeat completions add no progress. Different observing lengths and older formats may change the search cost per archive.
